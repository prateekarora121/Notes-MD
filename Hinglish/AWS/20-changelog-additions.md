## Summary of [resume-aligned] Restructure & Additions (This Pass)

### The restructure

Guide pehle conventional AWS-documentation order mein chalti thi (Compute → Storage → Networking → …), jo har service ko equal weight deti hai. Interviewers aisa nahi karte: wo us par sabse zyada drill karte hain jo resume **claim** karta hai, aur baaki ko skim karte hain. Isliye document ab char tiered parts mein organised hai, front mein [Resume-Aligned Priority Map](00-resume-aligned-priority-map.md#is-guide-ko-kaise-use-karein-resume-aligned-priority-map) navigation layer ke roop mein.

- **Part I — Tier 1: Resume-Claimed Core.** Wo topics jo mera resume explicitly naam leta hai (**Lambda, DynamoDB, EC2, S3, Terraform/CDKTF, GitHub Actions**) plus wo do jo unhe use karne mein unavoidable hain (**IAM, CloudWatch**). Yeh ab *pehle* aate hain, us order mein.
- **Part II — Tier 2: Design-Level Confidence.** Containers, relational databases/caching/analytics, networking, scalability, messaging, edge, security services — jahan reasoning operational history se zyada matter karti hai, aur jahan guide meri hands-on gaps explicitly state karti hai.
- **Part III — Tier 3: Breadth.** Management/billing, cost, migration, well-architected/DR — recognise karo, place karo, ek clean sentence.
- **Part IV — Cross-Cutting Reference.** Best practices, pitfalls, long-form Q&A, aur yeh changelogs.

1. **[Gap 1 — AWS Client VPN](11-networking.md#aws-client-vpn-individual-users-ko-vpc-ke-andar-laana)** — sabse bada gap, kyunki guide Client VPN ko Hybrid Connectivity ke aakhir mein **ek throwaway line** deti thi, jabki reference topology ka ALB internal-only hai, jisse Client VPN service tak *ekmatra* human raasta ban jaata hai. Aakhir mein platform team se kya maangna chahiye, jo khud ek senior-level answer hai.
3. **[Gap 3 — Internal vs internet-facing ALB, aur ALB ke subnet requirements](11-networking.md#internal-vs-internet-facing-alb-aur-alb-ke-subnet-requirements)** — `internal = true` **poore guide se absent** tha, jabki wahi reference topology ka poora ingress hai. Plus internal ALB ko phir bhi private-hosted-zone A-alias kyun chahiye, aur reference stack ke do deliberate-but-questionable choices: HTTP-only listener (honest in-VPC-encryption trade-off ke saath, aur yeh note ki ACM public certs internal ALBs par chalte hain) aur default action `fixed-response 501`, jo ek shared listener par achha design hai.
